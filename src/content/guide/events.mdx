An event is a budget with a start, an end and — usually — other people in it. A
weekend away, a birthday dinner, a road trip. You plan what it should cost,
track what it actually costs, and at the end Dotzie works out who owes whom.

This page covers planning and running. Finishing one is its own page:
[close an event and settle up](/guide/close-an-event).

## Create an event

**Add (+) → New Event**

### 1. Start from a preset (optional)

Tap **Presets** in the header for Trip, Road trip, Birthday, Party, Dinner,
Outing, Beach day and Wedding. Picking one pre-fills the setup so you're editing
rather than starting from nothing. You can save your own presets too, and edit
or delete them later.

### 2. Name it

Tap the emoji tile to change the icon, then give the event a name.

### 3. Set the dates

**Single day** or **Date range**.

### 4. Who's it for?

- **Just me** — a personal budget for the occasion.
- **Split it** — build the **People** roster. Add each person by name; they get
  a colour and an initials avatar. You're always in the roster as **You**.

### 5. Add the costs

For each planned cost:

- an emoji and a label — the field asks _What's this for?_
- an **amount**
- **who it's split with**
- a **split mode**: **equal**, or **custom** per-person amounts

Custom is there for the real cases — the person who isn't drinking, the couple
sharing a room, the friend who's only coming for one night.

### 6. Review and confirm

The **Review & confirm** step totals the plan **By person** or **By cost**, plus
a "Where it goes" breakdown. Check it reads the way you'd explain it out loud,
then tap **Confirm & save**. You can share the summary straight from this screen
— handy for putting the plan in the group chat before anyone's paid anything.

## Your events list

**Records → Events**, with tabs:

| Tab           | What's in it         |
| ------------- | -------------------- |
| **Current**   | Running right now    |
| **Upcoming**  | Planned, not started |
| **Finish up** | Over, but not closed |
| **Completed** | Closed and settled   |

**Finish up** is the one to watch. An event sits there until you close it, and
nothing reaches your ledger until you do.

## While the event is running

Open the event — there are two tabs, **budget** and **expenses**.

- Before the start date, the footer button reads **Start event**.
- Once running, the footer on the **expenses** tab becomes **Add an expense**:
  what it was for, the amount, and who it's split between.
- **Edit event** lets you rename it, change the icon or move the dates.
- **Add participant** brings someone in mid-event — for the friend who joins on
  day two.
- The **per-person view** shows what each person has paid and what they owe as
  you go, so nobody has to keep a mental tally.
- On the **budget** tab, the footer becomes **End event** when you're done.

> Add expenses _as they happen_, not at the end of the trip. The whole point of
> the per-person view is that the argument never starts — but it only works if
> the numbers go in while everyone still remembers who paid.

When the event's over, hit **End event** and carry on to
[closing it](/guide/close-an-event).
