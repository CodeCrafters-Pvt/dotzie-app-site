Most of this guide is about features. This page is about the thing underneath
them, because it explains why the app works the way it does.

## The short version

**Your data lives on your device, in a local database. There is no account, no
login, no server and no sync.** Dotzie doesn't have somewhere to send your
spending, so it doesn't.

The only way your data leaves the phone is a
[backup file you deliberately share](/guide/backup-restore) — and that file is
encrypted with a password only you know.

## What this buys you

- **Nothing to sign up for.** No email, no password, no verification. The app
  opens and works.
- **Nothing to leak.** There's no cloud copy of your finances to be breached,
  sold, subpoenaed or quietly analysed.
- **It works offline, always.** No connection needed, because there's nothing to
  connect to — on a plane, in a basement, abroad without data.
- **Your profile is yours.** Even your name and avatar stay put. The app says it
  plainly on that screen: _Nothing here leaves your device._

## What it costs you

Worth being straight about, because these are real:

- **No sync between devices.** Two phones means moving a
  [backup file](/guide/backup-restore) across by hand. There's no live shared
  ledger.
- **No recovery if you lose everything.** No backup file and password means the
  data is gone. Nobody can restore it for you — there's no copy to restore
  _from_.
- **Sharing is manual.** Sending an event summary to friends means using the
  **Share summary** button, not adding them to a shared account.

If those trade-offs sound wrong for you, that's fair — but they're the direct
consequence of the first section, not oversights. An app that can recover your
data for you is an app that has your data.

## Things Dotzie doesn't do

To be explicit, because other expense apps do these:

- It doesn't read your **SMS** or messages. There's no automatic capture of bank
  texts — [every entry is one you make](/guide/transactions), helped along by
  [templates](/guide/transactions) and
  [schedules](/guide/scheduled-transactions).
- It doesn't connect to your **bank**.
- It doesn't have **accounts, login or cloud sync**.
- It doesn't send **analytics** about your finances anywhere.
