The Dashboard is the screen you'll open most, so it's built to be rearranged.
Five cards, and you decide which ones you see and in what order.

## The cards

| Card              | What it shows                                  |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **This month**    | Your balance, and the month's money in and out |
| **Spending plan** | Budget progress as a donut                     |
| **Coming up**     | Upcoming scheduled expenses                    |
| **What you owe**  | You owe / owed to you totals                   |
| **Recent**        | Your latest transactions                       |

Tap through any card to get to the full screen behind it — **Recent** opens your
activity list, **Spending plan** opens the plan itself, and so on.

Pull down anywhere on the Dashboard to refresh.

## Rearranging it

1. Tap the **gear** icon on the Dashboard — the **Show & reorder** sheet opens.
2. Toggle a card **off** to hide it.
3. Drag a card by its **handle** to move it up or down.
4. Tap **Done**.

> A minimum number of cards has to stay visible, so you can't switch the whole
> Dashboard off. If a toggle refuses to turn off, that's why.

## Making it yours

There's no wrong arrangement, but a couple of setups people settle into:

- **Watching a budget?** Put **Spending plan** first and hide **What you owe**.
- **Splitting a lot with friends?** Put **What you owe** first — it's the number
  that changes most, and it links into [settling up](/guide/settling-up).
- **Just tracking?** **Recent** and **This month** at the top, everything else
  off, is a perfectly good dashboard.

Hiding a card only hides it here. The data stays exactly where it is, and the
full screen is still reachable from **Records** or **More**.
