Categories are how everything in [insights](/guide/insights) and
[spending plans](/guide/spending-plans) gets grouped, so they're worth picking
deliberately rather than reaching for _Other_ every time.

Every category picker in the app is **searchable** — type the first few letters
instead of scrolling the list.

## Expense

Food & Dining · Transportation · Shopping · Entertainment · Healthcare ·
Utilities · Housing · Education · Travel · Personal Care · Gifts · Other

## Income

Salary · Freelance · Investment · Business · Rental · Gift Received · Bonus ·
Other

## Scheduled expense

For [scheduled transactions](/guide/scheduled-transactions) going out:

Rent / Mortgage · Utilities · Subscriptions · Insurance · Loan Payment ·
Savings Transfer · Tuition / Fees · Memberships · Groceries · Other Scheduled

## Scheduled income

For scheduled transactions coming in:

Salary · Rental Income · Dividends · Pension · Freelance Retainer ·
Business Revenue · Refunds · Other Scheduled

## System categories

These are managed by the app itself. You'll see them on entries, but they don't
appear in the picker because Dotzie assigns them:

Loan Repayment · Loan Recovery · Event Spend · Event Payable · Event Receivable
· Account Balance

They're how [IOUs](/guide/settling-up) and
[closed events](/guide/close-an-event) show up in your ledger — if you spot one
on an entry you don't remember making, that's where it came from.

## A note on custom categories

Dotzie ships with the sets above, and they're what's available to pick today.
