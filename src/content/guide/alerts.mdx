The **Alerts** tab is an inbox inside the app, separate from your phone's
notifications. Things land here whether or not push notifications are switched
on — so turning notifications off means a quieter phone, not a missed budget
warning.

## The inbox

Alerts are grouped into **Today** and **Earlier**, and tagged by severity:

**Critical · Warning · Info** — and you can filter by these.

Per alert:

- **Mark as read** / **Mark as unread**
- **Remove**

In bulk:

- **Mark all read**
- **Remove all read**

Tap an alert to open its detail. Most alerts deep-link straight to the thing
they're about, so a budget warning takes you to the budget rather than leaving
you to find it.

## What actually creates an alert

Today, alerts come from your [spending plans](/guide/spending-plans):

- **Budget exceeded**
- **Approaching budget limit**
- **Budget period ended**

That's the full list. If your Alerts tab is quiet, it usually means you haven't
set up a spending plan yet — that's the feature that generates them.

> **Coming soon, per the app itself:** reminders for upcoming
> [scheduled transaction](/guide/scheduled-transactions) runs, and
> [IOU](/guide/settling-up) payment reminders. Both are shown as _coming soon_
> in Settings. Until they ship, use the **Coming up** card on your
> [dashboard](/guide/dashboard) to see what's due.

## Notification settings

**Settings → Notifications**

- **Push notifications** — the master switch. Everything below depends on it.
- **Logging** — _Log reminders_ ("Remind me to record transactions"), with
  **How often** and a **Reminder time**. If you're the kind of person who
  forgets to log for four days and then can't remember what you spent, this is
  the setting that fixes it.
- **Budget** — _Budget warnings_ with a **Warn me at** threshold percentage;
  _Budget updates_ when a plan ends or renews; and _Live budget_ (Android only)
  — an ongoing notification showing your current usage.

Permission for notifications themselves is granted in your phone's system
settings, not in Dotzie. If the switches here seem to do nothing, check there
first.
