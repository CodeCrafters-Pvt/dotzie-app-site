Every amount in Dotzie starts here. It's the flow you'll use dozens of times a
week, so it's worth two minutes to learn the shortcuts.

## Add a transaction

**Add (+) → Add Transaction**

1. Choose **Money out** or **Money in** with the toggle at the top — or just
   swipe left and right on the form.
2. Enter the **amount**.
3. **Pick a category.** The sheet that opens is searchable, so type rather than
   scroll. See [categories](/guide/categories) for the full list.
4. **A name for it** — the field is labelled _Paid to_ or _Came from_ depending
   on the direction (_Reliance Fresh_, _Salary, April_). Leave it blank and the
   category name is used instead.
5. **Date** — today by default. Future dates are allowed.
6. **Repeats** — One-off, Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Yearly.
7. **A little note** — optional, up to 500 characters.
8. Tap **Log it**.

> If you pick an expense category that isn't part of your active
> [spending plan](/guide/spending-plans), a one-line warning appears. It's a
> heads-up, not a block — dismiss it and save as normal.

### Repeats vs. scheduled transactions

The **Repeats** field on this form is the light-touch option: it marks the entry
as recurring and leaves it at that. If you want something that runs on its own
schedule, that you can pay early, skip, or end on a date, use a
[scheduled transaction](/guide/scheduled-transactions) instead. Rent, salary and
subscriptions belong there.

## Templates — for the entries you repeat

If you buy the same coffee every morning, don't type it every morning.

On the Add Transaction screen, tap **Templates** in the header:

- **Save this entry as a template** — fill in at least an amount and a category
  first, then give it a name. The template remembers the amount, category,
  description, note, direction (in/out) and repeat setting.
- **Tap any saved template** to fill the entire form instantly. Change what
  differs — usually just the amount — and log it.
- **Tap the trash icon** on a template to delete it. You'll be asked to confirm.

## Editing and deleting

Open any transaction from **Records → All activity** (or from **Recent** on the
[dashboard](/guide/dashboard)). The form reopens with the heading **Update
entry** and the button reading **Save changes**.

- **Delete** asks for confirmation first.
- The activity list also supports **bulk selection**, so you can clear out a run
  of entries in one go rather than one at a time.

## Finding things later

**Records → All activity** groups everything by day with a net figure per day,
and gives you:

- **Filters** — by type (all / money in / money out), by one or more categories,
  and by period (today, this week, this month, this year, or a custom date
  range), plus sorting.
- **Search** across your entries.

The metric boxes at the top of that screen always reflect the whole period, not
just the filtered rows — so the totals stay meaningful while you're digging
around inside a filter.
