**More → Settings.** Start with the **search bar** at the top — it's
typo-forgiving and ranked, and it jumps straight to an individual setting rather
than to the screen containing it. Faster than remembering which section anything
lives in.

Below it: a **profile chip**, **Quick settings** (dark mode, notifications
master toggle) and **All settings**.

## Profile

Tap the profile chip.

- **Name** — with a save button, and a guard that stops you leaving with unsaved
  changes.
- **Avatar** — a set of animal avatars, applied instantly with no save needed.

_Nothing here leaves your device_, as the screen says.

## General

- **Account balance** — _set to match what you actually have_. Opens the amount
  keypad. This is where you fix the balance if you skipped it during
  [onboarding](/guide/getting-started), or if it's drifted from reality.
- **Currency** — used across the app for every amount.
- **Date format** — how dates display.
- **Decimal places** — digits shown after the point.

## Appearance

- A live **Preview** card showing your colours and type on a sample amount and
  button, so you can see a change before you commit to it.
- **Dark mode** — follows your phone until you touch the switch, after which
  it's yours.
- **Accent colour** — nine palettes.
- **Type style** — font pairings for headings and body.

## Notifications

Covered in full on [Alerts and notifications](/guide/alerts).

## Sounds & haptics

- **Haptic feedback** — the small vibration on taps.
- **Sound effects.**

Both are worth turning off if you log transactions at your desk or in meetings.
