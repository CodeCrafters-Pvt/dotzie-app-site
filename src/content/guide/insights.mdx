**More → Insights** is where the entries you've been logging turn into
something you can actually read. Everything on this screen is calculated on your
phone from your own data — nothing is sent anywhere to produce it.

## Pick a period first

The period selector at the top drives the whole page:

**This week · This month · Last 3 months · This year · All time · Choose dates**

**Choose dates** opens a custom range — set the dates and tap **Apply range**.
Useful for a specific trip, a pay cycle that doesn't line up with the calendar
month, or the three weeks you'd rather not think about.

## What's on the page

### Summary

A plain-English headline sentence about the period, then **MONEY IN** and
**MONEY OUT** boxes and a line about what you saved.

### Ahead / Behind tracker

Whether you're pacing ahead of or behind your
[spending plan](/guide/spending-plans), as a progress bar with a detail line.
This is the one to check mid-month — being 60% through the budget on day 12 is
much easier to fix than finding out on day 30.

### Where your money went

Your top categories, each with an amount, a percentage and a bar. The fastest
way to answer "why was this month expensive?"

### Little insights

Short observations about your spending — the small patterns that are hard to
spot in a list of transactions.

## If it looks empty

> _Nothing to show yet — add a few transactions and your story will start taking
> shape here._

That's the empty state, and it means exactly what it says. Insights are built
entirely from what you've logged, so a quiet week here is a week of missing
entries rather than a bug. Give it a few days of real
[transactions](/guide/transactions) and the page fills in.

If the numbers look wrong rather than absent, the usual cause is a
[scheduled transaction](/guide/scheduled-transactions) set to **Fixed** with an
approximate amount — check those before you doubt the maths.
