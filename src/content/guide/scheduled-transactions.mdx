Rent, salary, the streaming subscription you keep meaning to cancel — anything
that comes round on a rhythm belongs here rather than being typed in every
month.

## Create a schedule

**Add (+) → Add Scheduled Transaction**

1. Choose **Money out** or **Money in**.
2. Pick an **Amount Type**:
   - **Fixed** — the same amount every time. Rent, a subscription, a salary.
   - **Variable** — you confirm the amount on each run. Electricity, phone
     bills, anything that moves.
3. Enter the amount, a description and a category.
4. Set the **recurrence** — including the **start date**, an **End Repeat**
   option if it shouldn't run forever, and **Add Date** for extra one-off dates
   on top of the pattern.
5. Tap **Save Schedule**.

You can also save a schedule as a **template**, the same way you can with a
[normal transaction](/guide/transactions), if you set up similar ones often.

> Choose **Variable** whenever the amount genuinely varies. A fixed schedule
> with an approximate amount quietly makes your
> [spending plan](/guide/spending-plans) and
> [insights](/guide/insights) wrong; a variable one asks you for the real
> number when it runs.

## Managing your schedules

**Records → Scheduled Transactions** lists everything you've set up, with search
and filters by type, status and date range.

Tap any schedule to open its detail sheet:

- **Pay now** — post this occurrence as a real transaction straight away. Useful
  when you've paid a bill early.
- **Skip** — skip a set number of occurrences, or skip until a date you choose.
  For the month the gym was closed, or a subscription you paused.
- **Edit** or **delete** the schedule.

If a search or filter leaves you with nothing, the empty state offers **Clear
search & filters** rather than making you undo each one.

## Where schedules show up

- **Coming up** on your [dashboard](/guide/dashboard) previews what's due.
- Once an occurrence is paid, it becomes an ordinary entry in **All activity**
  and counts toward your budgets and insights like anything else.

> Reminders for upcoming runs are marked **coming soon** in the app — schedules
> don't currently notify you before they're due. Keep the **Coming up** card
> visible if you want the warning.
