Closing is the step where an event stops being its own little world and becomes
part of your actual money. It's worth understanding, because _you_ decide what
gets written — nothing is recorded behind your back.

Tap **End event** on the event's budget tab to reach the **Close event** screen.

## What you're deciding

Two toggles, and each one writes something different.

### Money Out

Switch this on to record what _you_ paid out of pocket as a real expense entry
in your ledger.

- You can edit the description before it's written.
- A **preview** shows you the exact entry that will be created.
- If you didn't pay anything out of pocket, this section is hidden or disabled —
  there's nothing to record.

This is the one that makes the event show up in your
[insights](/guide/insights) and count against your
[spending plan](/guide/spending-plans).

### Your IOU records

Switch this on to log the payments that involve you as
[IOU records](/guide/settling-up) — what people owe you, and what you owe them.
Each one can be mapped to a new or an existing account.

If everyone's square, the screen simply tells you: _You're even with everyone,
nothing to log._

Then tap **Close event**.

> The two toggles answer different questions. **Money Out** is "what did this
> cost me?" — **Your IOU records** is "who still needs to pay whom?" A trip you
> paid for and will be reimbursed for wants both: the expense now, and the IOUs
> that cancel it out as people pay you back.

## After closing

You land on the **Event completed** screen:

- **Budget breakdown** — planned against actual.
- **Settlement plan** — who pays whom, worked out for you.
- **Per person** — the full picture for each participant.

**Share summary** sends it wherever you like. In practice this is the screenshot
that goes in the group chat, and it saves the conversation where four people
compare four different mental tallies.

## Deleting an event

Deleting is offered as **Delete event only** — and the wording is deliberate. It
removes the event, but **keeps the ledger entries it created**. Your expense
history and IOUs stay intact; you just lose the event that produced them.

If you want the ledger entries gone as well, delete those separately from
[All activity](/guide/transactions) and
[Settling up](/guide/settling-up).
