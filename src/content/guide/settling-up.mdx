An IOU is money that has changed hands but isn't finished — you covered dinner,
a friend spotted you a taxi, someone's paying you back in three parts. Dotzie
keeps these separate from your normal spending until they're settled.

## Track an IOU

**Add (+) → Track an IOU**

1. Choose **You owe** or **Owed to you** — toggle, or swipe.
2. Enter the **amount**.
3. **Who** — the person's name. (The provider if you owe; the debtor if you're
   owed.)
4. Pick a **category**.
5. Set the **start date** and the **due date**. The due date defaults to three
   days out and must be after the start date.
6. **Installments** — optional. Switch it on and set:
   - the **number of payments** (2 to 360),
   - a **frequency** — daily, weekly, monthly or custom,
   - the **first payment date**.
7. Add a description and note if you want, then save.

## The Settling up screen

**Records → Settling up** is organised by _person_, not by transaction — because
that's how you actually think about it.

- The **hero card** splits **OWED TO YOU** and **YOU OWE**. Tap either half to
  filter the list to that side.
- The **filter button** reveals search plus status pills — **All · Active ·
  Late · Settled** — each with a live count.

## Working through one person's records

Tap a person to open everything between the two of you. Inside:

- **Filter and search** within just their records.
- **Settle with &lt;name&gt;** — record a settlement against what's outstanding.
- **Even up** — this one only appears when you owe each other _both ways_. It
  nets the two sides off against each other instead of making you record two
  separate payments. If you don't see the button, the debt only runs one way.

Open a single record for its detail view:

- The full **installment schedule** with a status per payment.
- **Undo** on a payment you marked by mistake.
- **Reset to unpaid** to start the record's payments over.
- **Remove this** to delete the record entirely.

## How IOUs reach your ledger

Some IOUs are created for you rather than by you. When you
[close an event](/guide/close-an-event), Dotzie offers to log the payments
involving you as IOU records — so a trip you split with four people lands here
as who-owes-whom, ready to settle one at a time.
