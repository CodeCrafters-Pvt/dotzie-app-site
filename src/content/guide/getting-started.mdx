Dotzie opens straight into the app. There's no account to create, no email to
confirm and no password to remember — the only thing standing between you and
your first entry is a short welcome.

## Moving from another device?

The very first screen asks before anything else:

- **Restore my data** — jumps straight into the [restore flow](/guide/backup-restore).
  Your name and starting balance come back pre-filled from the backup, so you
  can skip the rest of this page.
- **Start fresh** — carry on below.

## Step 1 — Your name

Enter a name and tap **Continue**. It's only how the app addresses you; it isn't
a username and it never leaves your phone.

## Step 2 — Starting balance

This step is marked **Optional**, but it's worth doing:

1. Pick your **currency** from the picker.
2. Enter what you currently have on the amount keypad.
3. Tap **Get Started** — or **Skip for now**.

> Typing `0` is not the same as skipping. Zero records a real answer — "I have
> nothing right now" — while skipping leaves the balance unset. You can set it
> later at any time under **Settings → General → Account balance**.

## Where everything lives

Once you're through, you land on the five-tab app:

| Tab           | What it opens                                                                |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dashboard** | Your at-a-glance home                                                        |
| **Records**   | All activity · Scheduled Transactions · Settling up · Spending plan · Events |
| **Add (+)**   | New Event · Add Transaction · Add Scheduled Transaction · Track an IOU       |
| **Alerts**    | Your in-app notification inbox                                               |
| **More**      | Insights · Settings                                                          |

**Records** and **Add (+)** are menus rather than screens — they open a short
list and you pick from there. Almost everything in this guide starts from one of
those two.

## What to do next

Add one transaction. Genuinely — one real thing you spent money on today. The
[dashboard](/guide/dashboard), [insights](/guide/insights) and
[spending plans](/guide/spending-plans) all only come alive once there's
something to show, and the fastest way to see whether Dotzie fits you is to give
it a day of real entries.

When you've got a few, set up [backups](/guide/backup-restore). Because nothing
is stored on a server, a backup file is the only copy of your data that survives
a lost phone.
