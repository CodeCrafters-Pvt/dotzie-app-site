Most money apps ask you to hand over your entire financial life before they'll
track a single coffee. Dotzie takes the opposite bet: **your data never leaves
your phone.**

## What "offline-first" really means

Every expense, note, and category you enter is written to an encrypted database
on your device using **AES-256-GCM**. There's no Dotzie account, no cloud sync,
and no analytics SDK listening in the background.

- No account to create
- No network calls after install
- No servers that can be breached or subpoenaed

## Bring your data with you

When you want a backup, Dotzie exports a passphrase-encrypted file you control.
Save it to iCloud, Drive, or a USB stick — restore it on a new phone whenever
you like. The file is useless to anyone without your passphrase.

> Privacy isn't a feature we bolted on. It's the architecture.
